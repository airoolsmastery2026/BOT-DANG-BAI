# Quality gates

## Evidence

- Use only supplied or verified facts.
- Move missing facts to `verificationNeeded`.
- Block absolute, number-one, guaranteed, cheapest, lifetime, price, dimension, warranty, and performance claims without evidence.

## Channel fit

- Create a distinct hook, length, CTA, and format for each platform.
- Require public image URLs for Instagram and Pinterest jobs.
- Require public video URLs for TikTok and YouTube jobs.
- Default YouTube uploads to private until the operator explicitly changes privacy.

## Governance

- Keep platform tokens and provider API keys outside prompts and browser storage.
- Require QA score of at least 80 and explicit human approval before publishing.
- Require verified worker-vault accounts and a stable idempotency key.
- Treat analytics as proposed learning; require review before updating brand memory.
